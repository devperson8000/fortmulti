# Music-synchronized deployment

After every connected operator has entered a pod, doors seal and a 420 ms transition reaches full black. The supplied edited music starts there. HORIZON's existing transparent symbol reveals at the first adlib (10.000 seconds), fades out at “Wait” (20.190), and gives way to a camera orbit around the local operator's pod. The camera faces the actual Soldier character by “When” (23.550). The pod descends continuously to the operator's selected safe destination and touches ground at the beginning of “Big stepper” (25.580). The landed capsule stays anchored while the operator exits. A continuous camera arc converges on the actual first-person eye and heading, fading the local avatar before entering the head. The compact pod shell, planted feet, cyan rails and camera-facing fill light retain full framing at desktop, compact, ultrawide and portrait sizes. Nearby obstacles reserve space for the full camera orbit when choosing a safe destination. A locally completed exit retains its open shell and hidden HUD until the server confirms gameplay, so a late snapshot cannot flash a closed pod or snap the operator back inside. Gameplay overlays are hidden during the edit.

The music player schedules one AudioBufferSource per sequence. Camera, title and authoritative scripted deployment follow its audible device clock, using guarded output timestamps to account for render-ahead latency. Suspension cancels the frozen voice and recovery seeks the current cue; timestamp jitter cannot reverse the camera. Wall time remains the fallback when playback is unavailable or complete. Walking and combat retain the 50 ms physics cap. Crossing the entire landed stage in one delayed callback still emits touchdown once. Combat remains locked until pod exit. Hidden world rendering is skipped during full blackout.

The uploaded recording independently transcribes as “Game.” Unprompted recognition of the edited opening includes “in this game”; ASR mishears the preserved “stunts” word, which was not edited. The replacement spans 2.540–2.920 seconds with 12 ms crossfades and pitch-preserving time compression. Intermediate WAV duration and samples outside the replacement remain unchanged. The final stereo MP3 decodes to 26.680 seconds with a 650 ms tail fade. Extraction details and cue uncertainty are in `public/audio/README.md`.

Analysis used Whisper.cpp Small/base from CapSoftware's GitHub transcription-models distribution, Small DTW, PocketSphinx phone alignment and waveform measurements. First adlib timing is medium confidence; remaining spoken onsets have approximately 80 ms measurement uncertainty.
